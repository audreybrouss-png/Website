Malware and Cyber Threats

The Morris Worm of 1988

The Morris Worm was one of the first computer worms to gain widespread attention. Released in 1988, it spread rapidly across the internet, causing significant disruption and leading to the first conviction under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA).
